# Product

## Purpose

MoneyBook is a personal finance application for organizing accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, and reports. Success means users can understand their financial position and trace every account balance back to clear, auditable activity.

MoneyBook Lite is a lightweight companion for daily activity — recording withdrawals, deposits, and transfers quickly on a phone — while MoneyBook Web remains the advanced workspace for reports, budgets, goals, reconciliation, and statements.

## Users

Individuals and small-business users who need to understand account activity and balances while managing their finances.

## Brand personality

Warm, approachable, and trustworthy. The interface should make careful financial work feel understandable rather than intimidating.

## Design principles

- Make balances and the activity behind them easy to reconcile.
- Keep language approachable while preserving accounting accuracy.
- Money amounts are stored and displayed as integer cents to avoid precision loss.
- Transfers are never counted as income or expense.
- Keep financial records legible and usable across screen sizes.
- Reuse established MoneyBook workspace patterns for consistency.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Follow responsive behavior and standard accessible product conventions, including semantic controls, readable contrast, keyboard navigation, and meaningful labels.

## Non-goals (MoneyBook Lite)

Lite does not provide advanced reports, journal/audit workflows, reconciliation, backup/restore, detailed statements, or complex budget/goal administration. Users open MoneyBook Web for these features.
