# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals and small-business users who need to understand account activity and balances while managing their finances.

## Product Purpose

MoneyBook is a personal finance application for organizing accounts, transactions, budgets, and reports. Success means users can understand their financial position and trace account balances back to clear, auditable activity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, and trustworthy. The interface should make careful financial work feel understandable rather than intimidating.

## Anti-references

No strong user-specified anti-references. Avoid visual effects or interaction patterns that make financial information harder to scan or trust.

## Design Principles

- Make balances and the activity behind them easy to reconcile.
- Keep language approachable while preserving accounting accuracy.
- Reuse the established MoneyBook workspace patterns for consistency.
- Keep financial records legible and usable across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow existing responsive behavior and standard accessible product conventions, including semantic controls and readable contrast.
